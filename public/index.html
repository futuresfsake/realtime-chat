Before you connect

Adults only

This chat is for people 18 years old or older. If you are under 18, please close this page.

This is a preview

  • You are paired with one stranger at a time. They can read everything you send, and they can save or share it.
  • Messages and reactions are end-to-end encrypted: they are locked in your browser and only your partner's browser can unlock them. Our server passes them on but cannot read them.
  • This encryption has not been checked by an outside expert yet, and it depends on this page reaching you unmodified from our server. Do not write anything here that you could not afford to have exposed.
  • Do not share your name, phone number, address, school, workplace or social media accounts.

Rules

  • No harassment, threats or bullying.
  • No sexual harassment and no unwanted sexual messages (Safe Spaces Act, RA 11313).
  • Nothing sexual involving minors, ever. It is a crime and will be reported to the authorities (RA 11930).
  • No hate speech, spam, scams, impersonation or sharing other people's personal information.
  • You can report the person you are chatting with. The chat ends and you will not be paired with them again. Three reports from different people about the same connection block it from matching for 24 hours.

Privacy

  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads.
  • Your IP address is used for a moment to limit abuse. We keep only a scrambled (hashed) form of it, in memory, for up to 24 hours. It is never written to our logs.
  • Encrypted messages are passed on, kept in the server's memory (still encrypted) for up to two minutes so that a dropped connection can catch up, and then gone. Nothing is written to disk and we keep no chat history.
  • We cannot see what is said. We can still see that two connections are chatting, when, and roughly how much is sent. The "is typing" signal is not encrypted; it contains no text.
  • To match you, the server looks up your country (a two-letter code) from your IP address, then discards the address. That code and the interests you pick are held in the server's memory only while you search or chat, and are never written to logs. Country detection uses data from DB-IP.com.
  • Your partner sees which interests you have in common, and nothing else about you.
  • A report sends only the category you choose, never any messages (we cannot see them). While a report counts, the server holds the category and a scrambled form of both connections' addresses in memory for up to an hour, and writes the category and a short fragment of the reported connection's scrambled address to its logs. A block is held in memory for 24 hours. Reports are anonymous: the other person is only told the chat ended.
  • The server is hosted by Render in Singapore.
  • This acknowledgement is saved only in your own browser, for this visit.
