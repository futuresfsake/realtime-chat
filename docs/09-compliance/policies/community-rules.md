This is a chat between strangers. Be decent, and look after yourself.

## What is not allowed
| Not allowed | Examples | Related law ◻ |
|---|---|---|
| **Anything sexual involving a minor**, or trying to contact minors | Sexual messages to or about someone under 18; asking a young person to move to another app | RA 11930, RA 7610 |
| **Sexual harassment** | Unwanted sexual remarks, demanding or sending sexual images, sexist, homophobic or transphobic slurs | RA 11313 (Safe Spaces Act) |
| **Threats, intimidation and stalking** | "I will find you", wishing someone harm or telling them to harm themselves | Revised Penal Code; RA 10175 Sec 6 |
| **Hate speech** | Attacking people for who they are | Service rule |
| **Sharing other people's personal details** | Posting someone's number, address, school or accounts | RA 10173 |
| **Scams and impersonation** | Phishing, fake giveaways, pretending to be someone else | RA 10175 |
| **Trafficking or exploitation** | Recruiting or offering "work" that exploits people | RA 9208 as amended |
| **Spam and advertising** | Repeated messages, promotions, links to sell something | Service rule |
| **Getting around the rules** | Bots, scripts, evading blocks, switching networks to meet someone who left | Service rule |

## Staying safe
- **Do not share** your real name, phone number, address, school, workplace or social media accounts. The page warns you before you send something that looks like one.
- **You do not owe anyone a conversation.** Choose **Next** to meet someone new, or **End chat** to stop. People you leave are not paired with you again for 24 hours.
- **Anyone can screenshot a chat.** Write as if it could be shared.
- **Links are never clickable.** Do not copy one into your browser unless you are sure.
- If a message makes you uncomfortable, it may have been **hidden**; you choose whether to show it.

## Reporting
Choose **Report** at any time during a chat, pick what happened, and send. The chat ends and you will not be paired with that person again. We receive **only the category**, not the messages (we cannot see them). The other person is not told you reported them.

The categories are: harassment, unwanted sexual messages, hate speech, threats of harm, a person who seems to be under 18 or sexual content about a minor, spam or scams, and something else.

## What happens after a report
- When **three different people** report the same connection within an hour, that connection is **blocked from matching for 24 hours**, automatically.
- Reports about a possible minor or a threat of harm are **flagged** for priority attention. **[Human review: not built yet, see US-31.]**
- Conduct that may be a crime can be reported to the authorities. ◻

## If you were blocked by mistake
Blocks are automatic and last 24 hours. If you think it is wrong, write to **[contact email]**. On a shared network (a school, mobile data) a block can reach other people on the same network; that is a known limit.

## If something serious has happened to you
Report it in the chat, and then consider a complaint to the police. ◻ **[Confirm the current routes with counsel: PNP Anti-Cybercrime Group, NBI Cybercrime Division, and the helpline for child protection.]** Because we keep no messages, **take a screenshot yourself** if you want evidence.
