## 4. What we may do

We make these decisions automatically and cannot read your messages. If you believe a block is a mistake, contact **[contact email]**; we will look at it, but we may not be able to tell connections apart on a shared network.

You can report the person you are chatting with. We only receive the category you choose. Reports are anonymous, and the other person is not told. Please report honestly: deliberately false reports to harm someone break these terms.
