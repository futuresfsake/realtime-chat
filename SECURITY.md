# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability
Please **do not open a public issue**. Use GitHub's private "Report a vulnerability" (Security tab) on this repository.

We aim to acknowledge within 3 days and give a status update within 14 days. Please give us reasonable time to fix before disclosing.

## Scope
The deployed chat service and this repository. Out of scope: social engineering, denial-of-service load tests, third-party platforms (Render, GitHub).

## Safe harbour
Good-faith research that avoids other users' data and service disruption will not be pursued.

## Data protection concerns
Privacy or data-subject requests go to the designated Data Protection Officer (contact to be published with the privacy notice before launch).
